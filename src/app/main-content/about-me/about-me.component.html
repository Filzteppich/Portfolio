About me

Hi, I’m Khang.
What excites me about frontend development is the blend of logic, creativity, and problem‑solving. Coding feels limitless, and that’s what motivates me every day: you never stop learning — which makes this journey so rewarding. I want to grow, improve, and see what I’m capable of.
I live in Göttingen, Germany, and I prefer being part of a team where personal exchange is possible and I can learn from others. Remote work is also fine for me, but in‑person collaboration has special value.
Technology is constantly evolving — and I love growing with it. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are my current focus. JavaScript especially fascinates me: complex, challenging, and full of possibilities. That mix keeps me curious and motivates me to improve continuously.
I work analytically, creatively, and with structure. Clean, efficient, and readable code matters to me — not only to make things work, but so teams can build upon it together. Every project is a chance to find an elegant solution and grow.
